# Replace whitespace between code assignments
In the "Find what" field, enter: (\w+)\s*=\s*
	(\w+) captures one or more word characters (letters, numbers, underscore) as a group (the "name").
	\s* matches zero or more whitespace characters.
	= matches the equals sign literally.
In the "Replace with" field, enter: $1=
	$1 inserts the content of the first captured group (the "name").
	= inserts the equals sign.
